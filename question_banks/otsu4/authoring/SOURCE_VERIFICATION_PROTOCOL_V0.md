# Otsu4 Source Verification Protocol v0

Before an Otsu4 candidate is accepted, the author records its registered source ID, the source version above, and a pinpoint locator (law article/table, regulation article/table, or official exam-guide heading). A reviewer independently reopens that locator and confirms the original question, answer, and explanation are supported without copying protected past-question wording. Candidates with an unresolved locator or changed source version remain `AI_PRE_ACCEPT` and cannot receive an acceptance packet.

Published past questions are excluded: `O4-EXAM-1` may establish exam-profile facts only. No candidate may use it as the source of a question's wording, choices, answer, or explanation.
