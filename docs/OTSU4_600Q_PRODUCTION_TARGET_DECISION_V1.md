# Otsu4 600Q Production Target Decision v1

Status: **ADOPTED**
Decision date: 2026-08-25
Product: 危険物取扱者 乙種第4類 (Otsu4)

## Decision

The prior 300-question production target is superseded by a 600-question commercial production target.

- 法令: 260
- 基礎的な物理学及び基礎的な化学: 160
- 危険物の性質並びにその火災予防及び消火の方法: 180
- Total: 600 original accepted questions

This is a commercial positioning target, not a quota. It is intended to provide visibly deep coverage while remaining compatible with structured completion, weakness diagnosis, and subject-specific readiness.

## Quality floor

The 600 target does not relax any Question Factory gate.

- Do not reproduce or adapt copyrighted published past questions for commercial use.
- Every question must be original, source-traceable, and bound to an authoritative source/version/locator.
- No semantic filler, wording-only duplicate, or low-value variant may be accepted merely to reach 600.
- AI author, independent AI reviewer, and AI Director roles remain distinct.
- Acceptance packet, Permanent ID, canonical integration, source verification, and release remain separate gates.
- If authoritative source coverage cannot support 600 materially distinct questions, fail closed and record a coverage-limit decision rather than manufacture filler.

## Coverage direction

The product continues to use the three exam subjects as the top-level learning structure and the fifteen target families already defined by `question_banks/otsu4/QUESTION_BANK_CONTRACT_V0.md` as the internal coverage structure.

The next required planning step is to materialize a 600-question coverage map across those fifteen target families before broad authoring continues. The existing accepted candidate O4-B1-LAW-C002 remains valid; this target decision does not alter its reviewed content or acceptance evidence.

## Superseded decision

The original 300-question target remains durable historical evidence but is no longer the current production target.
